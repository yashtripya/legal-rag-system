=== THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA ===
Part III: Fundamental Rights

ARTICLE 14: EQUALITY BEFORE LAW
The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India.

ARTICLE 19: PROTECTION OF CERTAIN RIGHTS REGARDING FREEDOM OF SPEECH, ETC.
(1) All citizens shall have the right—
(a) to freedom of speech and expression;
(b) to assemble peaceably and without arms;
(c) to form associations or unions or co-operative societies;
(d) to move freely throughout the territory of India;
(e) to reside and settle in any part of the territory of India; and
(g) to practise any profession, or to carry on any occupation, trade or business.

(2) Reasonable Restrictions: Nothing in sub-clause (a) of clause (1) shall affect the operation of any existing law, or prevent the State from making any law, in so far as such law imposes reasonable restrictions on the exercise of the right conferred by the said sub-clause in the interests of the sovereignty and integrity of India, the security of the State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency or morality or in relation to contempt of court, defamation or incitement to an offence.

ARTICLE 21: PROTECTION OF LIFE AND PERSONAL LIBERTY
No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

JUDICIAL EXPANSION OF ARTICLE 21 BY THE SUPREME COURT:
The Supreme Court of India through landmark rulings (Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India 1978, Francis Coralie Mullin 1981, Olga Tellis 1985, Puttaswamy 2017) expanded Article 21 beyond mere animal existence to include:
1. Procedure must be fair, just, and reasonable (overruling A.K. Gopalan).
2. Right to live with human dignity.
3. Right to privacy.
4. Right to clean environment and free legal aid.
5. Right to livelihood and speedy trial.
